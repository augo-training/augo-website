## 3. What personal data do we process?

We process different categories of personal data depending on the occasion and purpose. You will find the most important categories below, whereby this list cannot be exhaustive.

You disclose much of the data mentioned in this section yourself (e.g. via the app, when communicating with us, in connection with contracts, when using the website, etc.). If you wish to enter into contracts with us or claim services, you must provide us with data as part of your contractual obligation under the relevant contract, in particular, master data and contract data. For the use of the app, processing of fitness, training and health data is essential.

You may provide us with data that relates to other individuals, such as credit card information, IP addresses, etc. If you do submit data concerning third parties, we understand that you confirm this data is correct and that you are authorised to provide us with this data. We ask you to inform these third parties about our processing of their data (for example, by a reference to this privacy policy).

### 3.1 Master data

Master data is the basic data that we need to process our business relationships or for marketing and advertising purposes and that relates directly to your person and characteristics. For example, we process the following master data:

- salutation, surname and first name, gender and date of birth;
- e-mail address;
- signature authorizations and consent forms.

We usually obtain this master data from you directly, but possibly also from third parties (e.g. family members or friends) who create an augo account for you.

We also obtain personal data from partner companies (e.g., Terra) and connected platforms (e.g., Garmin Connect, Strava, Rouvy, Polar, Wahoo, Coros, Zwift), provided that you give these partners and platforms the necessary consent to share the data with us. Please note that we are not responsible for data processing on any third-party platforms.

### 3.2 Training and fitness data

Our services are based on the processing of training and fitness data. In particular, our app allows you to record and document activities and related information as a coach or an athlete. For example, we process the following training and fitness data:

- athlete profile data such as experience level or weekly training hours;
- coach profile data such as sports coached, coaching experience, athlete count;
- information about completed workouts, including performance metrics;
- route and location data recorded during an activity (e.g. GPS tracks, including the start and end point of a session), where your recording device or a connected platform provides it to us;
- goals, achievements, milestones, information about races or other events;
- session feedback, including your feeling during the activity, any aches, pains or issues experienced, fuel and hydration intake, etc.;
- information about fitness assessments and test results, such as threshold power, threshold pace, and maximum and threshold heart rate.

### 3.3 Health data

Training and fitness data often encompasses or is related to health data. In particular, we process the following health data:

- health data that is provided to us via connected devices and platforms, in particular heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, daily step count and sleep (sleep duration, sleep stages, the time you fell asleep and woke up, and your resting heart rate during sleep), as well as the trends and baselines we derive from these values over time;
- body weight that you or your coach record;
- exercise-related injuries, pains, physical limitations, injury history (recent and persistent injuries).

### 3.4 Contract data

Contract data is information that incurs in connection with the conclusion or execution of a contract, for example information about contracts and the services to be rendered or the services rendered, as well as data from the period prior to the conclusion of a contract, information on the conclusion of the contract itself (e.g. the closing date and the subject matter of the contract), as well as the information required or used for the execution. For example, we process the following contract data:

- date, information on the type and duration as well as conditions of the respective contract, data concerning the termination of the contract;
- contact details;
- information on payments and payment methods, invoices, billing address, mutual claims, contact with customer service, objections, defects, returns, information on customer satisfaction, complaints, feedback, etc.;
- for services available online, also access data and logins.

We receive this data from you (i.e., when using the app, from athletes and coaches).

### 3.5 Communication data

Communication data is any data in connection with our communication with you, for example, when you contact us, when coaches and athletes communicate with each other via in-app messaging, via contact form, via customer support interactions or via other means of communication. Communication data is, for example:

- name and contact details such as e.g. postal address, e-mail address and telephone number;

If we record telephone conversations, we will inform you in each case at the beginning of the conversation. If you do not agree to the recording and storage of the call, you also have the option to end the call or contact us via other communication channels.

Technical data is generated in connection with the use of our website and the app. This includes, for example, the following data:

## 6. How do we use artificial intelligence (AI)?

We use AI to summarise conversations between athletes and coaches that use our app, and to highlight points in them that a coach may want to follow up on, for example when an athlete mentions pain, an injury or missed sessions. These highlights are shown only to the athlete's coach, as suggestions. The coach decides whether and how to act on them.

We also use AI to power features such as the AI assistant, coaching priorities, training summaries, and workout structuring. These features in particular process communication data, training and fitness data, and health data, including data we receive from connected platforms and devices such as Garmin (see section 17). In addition, we use AI to make our internal processes more efficient, enhance security, and prevent misuse.

The AI assistant and our other AI features only use your data if you have expressly allowed this (Article 9(2)(a) GDPR). This covers the training, fitness and health data we receive from your connected platforms and devices, your messages, and the photos and files you send. We ask for this permission separately from accepting our Terms: when you sign up or, if you already have an account, the next time you open the app. You can give or withdraw this permission at any time in your settings, and the change applies from that moment on. If you do not allow it, our AI features leave your data out entirely, and your coach is informed that AI insights are not available for you. Your activities, metrics and messages remain visible to you and your coach as usual.

We use data we receive from Garmin only to provide our AI features to you and your coach. We do not use it to train, test, evaluate or improve AI models, whether our own or those of our AI providers. This applies to the data in identifiable and in anonymised form, and to conversations, prompts and summaries that contain it.

Our AI features are powered by AI models from third-party AI providers, some of which process data in the USA (see section 11). The data required for a given feature is transmitted to the respective provider for processing under a data processing agreement. The provider does not use it to train its models and does not retain it beyond what is needed to process the request. We use Langfuse to record the inputs and outputs of our AI features so that we can detect and correct errors, and we keep these records only for a limited period. All of these providers process personal data exclusively according to our instructions as processors (see section 10).

When you send a voice message in the chat, we have it transcribed automatically so that it can also be read as text. For this, the recording is transmitted to ElevenLabs, a speech recognition service that acts as our processor and may process data in the USA (see section 11). The transcript is stored with the message, is visible to the participants of the chat, and is used by our AI features in the same way as written messages.

Where you have given the permission described above, our AI also reads photos and files (such as PDFs) that you send in the chat, so that your coach and the AI assistant can work with their content. It creates a written description of the training-related content, such as values from a chart or the steps of a training plan, and does not identify or describe people shown in an image. We store this description with the message and delete it when the message is deleted. Without your permission, our AI does not read your photos and files. Coaches can also upload photos and files to the AI assistant directly; these are read in the same way and stored with the coach's assistant conversation.

Photos and files can contain health data, for example about an injury, or data about other people. Please only send what you are comfortable sharing with your coach and our AI features, and only send images of other people with their consent. Descriptions created by AI can be incomplete or wrong, so your coach should check the original before relying on it.

## 7. What online tracking and online advertising techniques do we use?

On our website, we use various techniques to let us and third parties consulted by us recognise you when you use our website and, in some cases, track you across multiple visits. The use of such techniques is regulated separately. The following section provides information on this topic.

### 7.1 How and for what purpose do we use cookies and similar technologies?

We use third-party services for our website to measure and improve the user experience of the website and online advertising campaigns. For this purpose, we may embed third-party components on our website, which may in turn use cookies. When we track you or use similar technologies, the core purpose is to enable us to distinguish access by you (via your system) from access by other users so that we can ensure the functionality of the website and perform statistical analyses. We do not want to identify you in this process. The technology used is designed to recognise you as an individual visitor each time you access the site, for example, by having our server (or the servers of third parties) assign a specific identification number to you or your browser (so-called "cookie").

Cookies are files that your browser automatically stores on your end device when you visit our website. Cookies contain a unique identification number (an ID) that allows us to distinguish individual visitors from others, but usually without identifying them. Depending on the purpose use, cookies contain further information, for example, on accessed sites and the duration of the visit to a site. On the one hand, we use session cookies, which are deleted again when the browser is closed, and on the other hand, we use permanent cookies, which remain stored for a certain duration after the browser is closed and are used to recognise visitors on a subsequent visit.

We use the following types of cookies and similar technologies:

- Necessary cookies: necessary cookies are required for the functionality of the websites, for example, to allow you to switch between sites without losing information entered in a form.
- Performance cookies: these cookies collect information about the use of a website and enable analyses, for example, which sites are most popular. They can thereby simplify the visit to a website and improve the user experience.
- Functional cookies: functional cookies enable advanced features and can display personalised content.
- Marketing cookies: marketing cookies help us and our advertising partners to target you on our websites and on websites of third parties with advertisements for products or services that may be of interest to you or to display our advertisements during your further internet use after visiting our websites.

We particularly use cookies for the following purposes:

- personalisation of content;
- displaying personalised advertisements and offers;
- displaying ads on third-party websites and measuring their success, i.e. whether you respond to these ads (remarketing);
- save settings between your visits;
- determining whether and how we can improve our website;
- collection of statistical data on the number of users and their usage habits, as well as to improve the speed and performance of the website;
- we may process your contact details to target you with advertisement on third-party platforms.

We may also use similar technologies (e.g. LinkedIn Insight Tags, Pixel Tags or Fingerprints) to store data in the browser. Pixel Tags refer to small, usually invisible images or a program code that are loaded from a server and thereby transmit certain information to the operator of the server, e.g. whether and when the website was visited. Fingerprints are information that is collected when you visit our website via the configuration of your end device or your browser, and which make your end device distinguishable from other devices.

### 7.2 How can cookies and similar technologies be disabled?

When accessing our website, you have the option to activate or deactivate certain categories of cookies. You can configure your browser in the settings to block certain cookies or similar technologies or delete existing cookies and other data stored in the browser. You can also enhance your browser with software (so-called "plug-ins") that blocks tracking by certain third parties. You can find out more about this in the help pages of your browser (usually under the heading "Privacy"). ## 17. Connected platforms and devices

You can connect third-party platforms and devices to your augo account in order to bring your activity, training and health data into the platform, either directly (Garmin, see sections 17.1 to 17.6) or via Terra, a data aggregation service (Apple Health, Oura, Whoop, Wahoo, Strava, Polar, Coros, Zwift and others, see sections 17.7 to 17.11). We receive this data only after you have granted the necessary authorisation on the platform concerned, and you can withdraw that authorisation at any time, either there or in the app.

### 17.1 Garmin: what data we collect and how

You can connect your Garmin account to augo directly. When you do so, you are redirected to Garmin, where you log in and authorise augo to access your data. augo only receives the data covered by the permissions you grant in Garmin, and Garmin transmits new and updated data to us automatically once you have connected your account. Garmin decides which data it makes available to apps such as augo and may change this, so some metrics shown on your Garmin device may not be available in augo.

Depending on the permissions you grant, we receive the following data from Garmin:

- activities and activity details, such as the type of sport, start time, duration, distance, pace, speed, power, heart rate, cadence, laps, and the GPS route;
- information about planned workouts that we have sent to your Garmin device and their completion status;
- health metrics. Garmin transmits the health metrics covered by the permissions you grant, which may include daily heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), sleep, steps, stress, body composition and, where you have enabled it in Garmin, menstrual cycle tracking data. Of these, we currently store and use resting heart rate, HRV, sleep and daily step count, together with the trends and baselines we derive from them (see section 17.2), and discard the other categories on receipt. Should we start using further categories, we will update this section and inform you beforehand.

### 17.2 Garmin: what we use it for

We use the data we receive from Garmin exclusively to provide our services, namely:

- to show your activities and metrics to you and to your coach;
- to calculate training load, fitness, fatigue, training zones and similar metrics;
- to calculate trends and baselines of your resting heart rate, HRV and sleep over time, so that you and your coach can see how your recovery develops;
- where you have allowed this, to generate answers from the AI assistant, coaching priorities, summaries and other AI-supported insights for you and your coach (see section 6);
- to send workouts planned in augo to your Garmin device.

We do not use Garmin data for advertising or marketing purposes, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train, test, evaluate or improve AI models (see section 6).

### 17.3 Garmin: how it is processed and stored

The data we receive from Garmin is stored in our database and file storage on our cloud platform, which is located in the European Union. Data is transmitted to us and between our systems in encrypted form. We keep the raw activity data as received from Garmin so that we can reprocess it, for example when we improve the metrics we calculate from it.

### 17.4 Garmin: who else processes it

Data we receive from Garmin is visible to the coach you are connected with in augo (see section 10). Beyond this, it is processed by the following service providers, each of which acts as our processor, is bound by contract to process the data exclusively according to our instructions, and does not use it for its own purposes:

- our cloud hosting provider, which operates the infrastructure described above;
- third-party AI providers, whose AI models power the AI features described in section 6 and which receive the training and health data, including data derived from Garmin, that a given feature requires;
- Langfuse, which records the inputs and outputs of our AI features so that we can detect and correct errors;
- Sentry, our error monitoring service (see section 7.3).

The product analytics data we collect with Mixpanel (see section 7.3) does not include Garmin data or metrics derived from it. We do not share Garmin data with any other third party.

### 17.5 Garmin: disconnecting and deletion

You can disconnect your Garmin account at any time, either in the app or by revoking augo's access in your Garmin account. From that moment we no longer receive any data from Garmin. Activities and metrics that were imported before you disconnected remain part of your training history in augo. They are deleted when you request the deletion of your augo account (see section 15).

### 17.7 Platforms connected via Terra: what data we collect and how

Apple Health, Oura, Whoop, Wahoo, Strava, Polar, Coros, Zwift and a number of other platforms are connected to augo via Terra (tryterra.co), a data aggregation service based in the United Kingdom that acts as our processor. When you connect one of these platforms, you log in there and authorise access to your data; Terra then receives the data covered by that authorisation and forwards it to us. For Apple Health, the augo app asks iOS for read access to your workout data, and Terra's software component inside the app forwards that data to Terra and on to us. augo never writes to Apple Health.

From platforms connected via Terra we receive the following data:

- activities and activity details, such as the type of sport, start time, duration, distance, pace, speed, power, heart rate, calories and the GPS route, from Apple Health, Wahoo and the other connected platforms;
- daily health metrics from the connected platforms that provide them (for example Apple Health, Oura, Whoop, Polar or Coros), currently resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), daily step count and sleep (sleep duration, sleep stages, the time you fell asleep and woke up, and your resting heart rate during sleep).

We only request from Terra the data categories we use for the features described in section 17.8, and we do not store data categories we do not use. We may extend the metrics we receive as we add features; where we do, we will update this section.

### 17.8 Platforms connected via Terra: what we use it for

We do not use data received via Terra for advertising, marketing or data mining purposes, and we do not sell it. Data from Apple Health is not disclosed to any third party other than the processors listed in section 17.10, and only for the purpose of providing coaching to you. ### 17.9 Platforms connected via Terra: how it is processed and stored
